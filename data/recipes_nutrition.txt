=== BREAKFAST RECIPES ===

RECIPE: Oatmeal with Banana and Honey
Category: Breakfast, Weight Loss
Preparation Time: 10 minutes
Serving Size: 1 bowl

Ingredients:
- 1/2 cup rolled oats
- 1 cup water or milk
- 1 medium banana, sliced
- 1 tablespoon honey
- Pinch of cinnamon

Instructions:
1. Boil water or milk in a pot.
2. Add oats and reduce heat to medium.
3. Cook for 5 minutes, stirring occasionally.
4. Top with sliced banana, honey, and cinnamon.

Nutritional Information (per serving):
- Calories: 285 kcal
- Protein: 7g
- Carbohydrates: 55g
- Fat: 4g
- Fiber: 5g
- Sugar: 22g

Best For: Weight loss, High fiber diet, Heart health

RECIPE: Vegetable Omelette
Category: Breakfast, High Protein
Preparation Time: 15 minutes
Serving Size: 1 omelette

Ingredients:
- 2 large eggs
- 1/4 cup diced bell peppers
- 1/4 cup diced onions
- 2 tablespoons diced tomatoes
- Salt and pepper to taste
- 1 teaspoon olive oil

Instructions:
1. Beat eggs in a bowl with salt and pepper.
2. Heat oil in a non-stick pan over medium heat.
3. Saute vegetables for 2 minutes.
4. Pour eggs over vegetables.
5. Cook until set, then fold in half.

Nutritional Information (per serving):
- Calories: 195 kcal
- Protein: 14g
- Carbohydrates: 5g
- Fat: 13g
- Fiber: 1g
- Cholesterol: 370mg

Best For: High protein diet, Muscle building, Low carb diet

=== LUNCH RECIPES ===

RECIPE: Grilled Chicken Salad
Category: Lunch, Weight Loss, High Protein
Preparation Time: 25 minutes
Serving Size: 1 large bowl

Ingredients:
- 150g chicken breast
- 2 cups mixed greens
- 1/2 cucumber, sliced
- 10 cherry tomatoes
- 1/4 red onion, sliced
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 tablespoon lemon juice
- Salt, pepper, and herbs to taste

Instructions:
1. Season chicken with salt, pepper, and herbs.
2. Grill chicken for 6-7 minutes each side until cooked.
3. Let rest for 5 minutes, then slice.
4. Arrange greens, cucumber, tomatoes, and onion in a bowl.
5. Top with sliced chicken.
6. Drizzle with olive oil and lemon juice.

Nutritional Information (per serving):
- Calories: 385 kcal
- Protein: 42g
- Carbohydrates: 12g
- Fat: 18g
- Fiber: 4g
- Sodium: 320mg

Best For: Weight loss, Muscle building, Low carb diet

RECIPE: Vegetable Stir Fry with Tofu
Category: Dinner, Vegan, Low Calorie
Preparation Time: 25 minutes
Serving Size: 1 plate

Ingredients:
- 150g firm tofu, cubed
- 1 cup mixed vegetables (broccoli, snap peas, carrots, bell peppers)
- 2 tablespoons soy sauce (low sodium)
- 1 tablespoon sesame oil
- 1 teaspoon ginger, minced
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1/2 cup brown rice (cooked)

Instructions:
1. Press tofu to remove excess water, then cube.
2. Heat sesame oil in a wok over high heat.
3. Add tofu and cook until golden (5 minutes).
4. Add garlic and ginger, cook 1 minute.
5. Add vegetables and stir fry for 5-7 minutes.
6. Add soy sauce and toss.
7. Serve over brown rice.

Nutritional Information (per serving):
- Calories: 380 kcal
- Protein: 22g
- Carbohydrates: 35g
- Fat: 16g
- Fiber: 6g
- Iron: 4mg

Best For: Vegan diet, Weight loss, Plant-based protein

# ===================== IMPORTS =====================
import streamlit as st
import chromadb
from sentence_transformers import SentenceTransformer
import requests
import os
from dotenv import load_dotenv

# ===================== LOAD ENV ====================
load_dotenv()
GROQ_API_KEY = os.getenv("GROQ_API_KEY")

# ===================== PAGE CONFIG =================
st.set_page_config(
    page_title="Recipe & Nutrition Chatbot",
    page_icon="🥗",
    layout="wide"
)

# ===================== LOAD CSS ====================
if os.path.exists("styles.css"):
    with open("styles.css") as f:
        st.markdown(f"<style>{f.read()}</style>", unsafe_allow_html=True)

# ===================== EMBEDDINGS ==================
@st.cache_resource
def load_embedder():
    return SentenceTransformer("all-MiniLM-L6-v2")

# ===================== CHROMA DB ===================
@st.cache_resource
def get_collection():
    client = chromadb.PersistentClient(path="./chroma_db")
    collection = client.get_or_create_collection("recipes")
    return client, collection

client, collection = get_collection()
embedder = load_embedder()

# ===================== LLM FUNCTION ================
def ask_nutrition_bot(question, context):
    system_prompt = """You are an English-only Recipe and Nutrition Chatbot.

STRICT RULES:
1. Communicate ONLY in English. Never use any other language.
2. Answer questions ONLY using the provided context below.
3. Do NOT use general knowledge, assumptions, or internet-based information.
4. Do NOT invent recipes, diet plans, or nutritional values.
5. If information is not in the context, respond exactly with:
   "The requested information is not available in the current recipe and nutrition dataset."
6. Maintain a clear, professional, and helpful tone.
7. For medical questions, state: "This chatbot provides general nutrition information only. Please consult a healthcare professional for medical advice."

CONTEXT FROM DATABASE:
{context}

FORMAT YOUR RESPONSES:
- Use clear headings and bullet points
- Include nutritional values when available
- Mention serving sizes
- Be concise but complete"""

    response = requests.post(
        "https://api.groq.com/openai/v1/chat/completions",
        headers={
            "Authorization": f"Bearer {GROQ_API_KEY}",
            "Content-Type": "application/json"
        },
        json={
            "model": "llama-3.1-8b-instant",
            "messages": [
                {"role": "system", "content": system_prompt.format(context=context)},
                {"role": "user", "content": question}
            ],
            "temperature": 0.2
        }
    )
    return response.json()["choices"][0]["message"]["content"]

# Show chat messages
for msg in st.session_state.messages:
    avatar = "🧑" if msg["role"] == "user" else "🥗"
    with st.chat_message(msg["role"], avatar=avatar):
        st.markdown(msg["content"])

# Chat input
prompt = st.chat_input("Ask about recipes, nutrition, or diet plans...")

if prompt:
    # Add user message
    st.session_state.messages.append({"role": "user", "content": prompt})
    
    with st.chat_message("user", avatar="🧑"):
        st.markdown(prompt)
    
    with st.chat_message("assistant", avatar="🥗"):
        if chunk_count == 0:
            response = "⚠️ Knowledge base is empty. Please run `python setup_knowledge.py` first."
        else:
            # Search in database
            query_embedding = embedder.encode(prompt).tolist()
            results = collection.query(
                query_embeddings=[query_embedding],
                n_results=5
            )
            
            if results["documents"][0]:
                context = "\n\n---\n\n".join(results["documents"][0])
                response = ask_nutrition_bot(prompt, context)
            else:
                response = "The requested information is not available in the current recipe and nutrition dataset."
        
        st.markdown(response)
        st.session_state.messages.append({"role": "assistant", "content": response})
